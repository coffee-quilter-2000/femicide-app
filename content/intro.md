**Femicide** is the killing of women and girls, most often because they are women. The World Health Organization describes it as the intentional killing of women, and notes that a large share of these killings are committed by a current or former intimate partner.

The United States has no official femicide count, so this project builds one from the FBI's Supplementary Homicide Reports and looks at how it moves alongside poverty, health insurance coverage and education.
